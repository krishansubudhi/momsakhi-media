Fun project alert 🪐

Try a Play-Doh solar system with your kid!

My son and I rolled the 8 planets, made orbit rings with thin dough strips, and put them in order from the sun.

He chose every color himself (the purple planet with the yellow ring is VERY important 💜💛).

What kids learn while playing:
🌞 the order of the planets
🪐 which planet is biggest and smallest
🎨 colors, shapes and fine motor skills

All you need is Play-Doh and 20 minutes.
